The six language icons in the home-page Get started block (Java, Python, Go,
C#/.NET, Rust and Node.js) come from the Symbols icon theme by Miguel Solorio.

Source: https://github.com/miguelsolorio/vscode-symbols
Files: src/icons/files/java.svg, python.svg, go.svg, csharp.svg, rust.svg and
node.svg, as published in version 0.0.21.

Modifications: extracted each icon's paths into an inline SVG sprite, changed
the fill and stroke colours to currentColor, and renamed internal mask and
clipPath ids so they stay unique on the page. No path data was edited.
