Speedle is an open-source (UPL-1.0) authorization engine with the SPDL policy language. It consists of ADS (decisions) and PMS (policy management), SDKs in six languages, and Speedle AI (authorization for and with AI agents, an MCP server, and the policy-modeler skill).

Speedle was built by the team behind Oracle Entitlements Server (OES). Its design draws on many years of that team's work on earlier authorization products, including OES, BEA ALES/WLES and CrossLogix.

The open source version of Speedle consists of:

- SPDL (Speedle Policy Definition Language)
- Policy Management Service
- Authorization Decision Service
- Command line tool (spctl)
